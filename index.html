Become a Travel YouTuber!

A WebQuest for 5th grade. Choose a place, give advice and share it with the world.

Introduction

Have you ever wanted to be a YouTuber? Now is your chance!

Imagine you are a travel YouTuber and you want to recommend an amazing place to your followers. Your mission is to create your own YouTube video and show everyone a place they should visit!

You can talk about a beautiful beach, a big park, a green mountain, a famous volcano or amazing cliffs. Tell us what people can visit, see, eat, do and where they can stay.

Don't forget to use the word "should" to give advice. For example:
"You should visit this beautiful beach!"

Be creative, have fun and get ready to share your favourite place with the world. Lights, camera, action!

Objectives

By the end of this task, you will be able to:

  • 🏔️ Use the new vocabulary and language. mountain, beach, volcano, park, cliffs… and should / shouldn't.
  • 🗣️ Give clear advice to your viewers.
  • 🎬 Be creative and make an effort in your final video.

Steps to follow

Follow the steps in order. Ask your teacher if you need help!

  1. Choose a place and write your recommendations.
  2. Follow the tutorial to create your YouTube profile. Open YouTube tutorial
  3. Record your video using the ideas you wrote.
  4. Edit your video with the CapCut app. Open CapCut tutorial
  5. Upload your final video to YouTube.

Conclusion

Great job, travel YouTubers!

By doing this activity, you have learned how to recommend places to other people and help them discover new places to visit. Your videos can help people learn about different places and understand why it is important to take care of nature and keep our beaches, mountains, parks and volcanoes clean.

You have also learned new words in English, how to describe places with adjectives and how to give advice using should. In addition, you have practised speaking English, sharing ideas, using technology and being creative.

Remember, your words can help other people discover the world and protect our planet! 🌍
